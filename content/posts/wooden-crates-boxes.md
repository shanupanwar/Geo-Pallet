Some products need more protection than a standard pallet can provide. That’s why many businesses use wooden crates and boxes for shipping heavy, fragile, or high-value items.

Wooden crates are built to protect products during transport, storage, and export shipping. They help reduce movement, absorb impact, and provide stronger protection when products are being handled multiple times during shipping.

**Businesses across Vancouver, Langley, and throughout British Columbia commonly use crates and boxes for:**

-   Machinery
-   Automotive parts
-   Electronics
-   Industrial equipment
-   Export shipments
-   Fragile products

Our **wooden crates and boxes** are manufactured using strong lumber and durable construction methods designed for heavy-duty shipping conditions.

For international transport, we also build **heat-treated ISPM 15** compliant crates and boxes that meet export regulations. These export-ready packaging solutions help businesses avoid customs problems and shipping delays when transporting products outside Canada.

**Wooden crates** can be fully enclosed or partially open depending on the product and shipping requirements. Some businesses need maximum protection from weather and handling damage, while others prefer open-style crates for easier inspection and loading access.

Custom sizes are also available for oversized or unusually shaped products that do not fit standard packaging dimensions.

Another advantage of wooden crates is stackability. Properly built crates help improve warehouse organization and make container loading more efficient during shipping.

For businesses shipping expensive equipment or fragile products, stronger packaging often saves money in the long run by helping reduce product damage during transport.
