Not every business needs brand-new pallets for every shipment. That’s one reason recycled wooden pallets continue to be a popular option for warehouses, logistics companies, and manufacturers across British Columbia.

**Recycled pallets** are previously used pallets that have been inspected, repaired, and prepared for reuse. They provide a lower-cost shipping solution while still offering dependable performance for many warehouse and transportation applications.

Businesses in Surrey, Vancouver, and Langley often choose recycled pallets because they help reduce shipping costs without sacrificing functionality.

#### Recycled wooden pallets are commonly used for:

-   Warehousing
-   Retail shipments
-   Manufacturing
-   Logistics operations
-   Distribution centers

Before being reused, pallets are checked for structural issues and repaired where needed. Damaged deck boards, stringers, and weak sections are replaced to help maintain safe handling and load support.

Many businesses also choose recycled pallets because they support sustainability efforts by reducing wood waste and extending pallet life.

**Heat-treated recycled pallets** are also available for export applications that require ISPM 15 compliance.

Depending on inventory and application needs, businesses can choose between different **recycled pallet grades.**
