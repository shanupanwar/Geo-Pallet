Our Story

Why Geo Pallet Began

Built to Meet a Need in B.C.
Geo Pallet Ltd. was created to solve a common challenge across British Columbia: businesses needed strong, reliable, custom wooden pallets delivered on time and built with consistent quality.

Founding of Geo Pallet Ltd. (2007)

Founded by Industry Leaders
Geo Pallet Ltd. was established in 2007 by Arshad Latif and Muhammed Ezaz Qazi, combining extensive hands-on experience in pallet manufacturing.

Mr. Latif previously owned a well-known pallet making company in British Columbia.Together  they built Geo Pallet with a focus on quality, dependability, and long term customer trust

Building the Foundation

How We Built Trust and Strengthened Operations
With years of experience behind them, the founders grew Geo Pallet through:

  • Hands-on operations and strong shop-floor discipline
  • Long-term relationships with customers and suppliers
  • Continuous improvements in production, quality control, and delivery planning

Inheritance and Active Involvement

Continuing the Legacy Leading Today
Hammadullah Qazi and Omar Arshad inherited both the company and the industry expertise behind it. Today, they are actively involved in:

  • Company direction and strategic decisions
  • Daily management and operational oversight
  • Maintaining high standards across B.C. facilities

The Gaps We Solved

From Industry Gaps to Engineered Solutions
Geo Pallet was built to address real, everyday challenges businesses faced:

  • Unreliable timelines and late deliveries

Logo Rationale

Our logo started with the Maple Leaf to represent our Canadian roots and the care we put into our work. As we grew, we added more elements to tell our story. The upward graph represents growth, while the Three Sisters Mountains reflect the values we stand by: trust, kindness, and confidence. Together, these details show our journey, our progress, and the kind of impact we want to make.
