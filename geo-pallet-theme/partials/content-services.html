  • Heat Treated Pallets (ISPM-15 Compliant)

Geo Pallet Ltd. provides ISPM-15 compliant heat-treated pallets to help businesses ship internationally with fewer delays at the border. Heat treatment is required in global trade to reduce the risk of spreading wood pests through packaging materials.Our ISPM-15 process: We purchase pre-heat-treated lumber from certified mills and apply the HT stamp after the pallets are assembled, ensuring pallets are export-ready.

  • Approved ISPM-15 treatment options include
Heat Treatment (HT):

minimum 56°C core temperature for 30 minutes

Methyl Bromide (MB) fumigation

Note: ISPM-15 applies to both softwood (coniferous) and hardwood (non-coniferous) wood packaging materials.
