2. Stringer Wooden Pallets

Stringer wooden pallets are one of the most common choices for shipping and warehouse use. They are built with parallel stringers that support the deck boards, giving the pallet a solid structure for regular handling and storage. These wooden shipping pallets can be made in different sizes and load capacities depending on what your business needs. They are available in both 2-way and 4-way entry styles, making them useful for many warehouse layouts and forklift setups.

As a custom pallet manufacturer, we can adjust the lumber, spacing, size, and strength of stringer pallets to match your products and shipping process.

Two-Way Stringer Wooden Pallet

A two-way stringer wooden pallet allows forklift entry from the front and back. This makes it a simple and dependable option for businesses that need straightforward handling and storage.

Two-way pallets are often used when products move in a set direction through the warehouse. They can also be built as reversible pallets with deck boards on both sides, giving extra support when stacking heavier loads.

3-Block Wooden Pallets

Block wooden pallets are built with solid blocks between the top and bottom deck boards. This design gives the pallet strong support and allows forklift access from all four sides. Block pallets are a good choice for heavier loads, warehouse racking, and industrial shipping. They offer better stability than many basic pallet styles and are often used when products need extra support during transport. Many companies looking for heavy duty pallets choose block designs because they offer strong weight distribution and long-lasting performance.

4-Wing Wooden Pallets

Wing wooden pallets are available in single-wing and double-wing styles. A single-wing pallet has deck boards that extend beyond the stringers or blocks on the top only, while a double-wing pallet extends on both the top and bottom. This extra surface area helps support wider or uneven loads. Wing pallets are often used in industries such as automotive, metal, electronics, and manufacturing. For better forklift access, wing pallets can also be built with notched stringers. Businesses in Langley and Richmond often use this style for products that need more surface support during shipping.

5-Wooden Skids

Wooden skids are a simple and useful alternative to traditional pallets. They are built with parallel stringers and top deck boards, but they do not have bottom deck boards. Because of this design, skids are mainly used for horizontal movement rather than stacking. They work well for heavy machinery, equipment, temporary platforms, and industrial products that need stable ground support. Skids are often used in construction, manufacturing, and equipment transport where strength matters more than standard pallet stacking.
